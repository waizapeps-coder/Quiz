Punctuating Direct Speech

Year 9 Worksheet · Technology: a Blessing or a Hindrance?

📘 Remember: Rules for Direct Speech

  • Quotation marks: double marks around the spoken words only, not around the reporting verb (asked, replied, said).
  • Reporting verb at the end: the comma, question mark or exclamation mark goes inside the closing quotation marks, with no capital after it unless it is a name.
  • Split in the middle of a sentence: the second part begins with a small letter.
  • Split between two sentences: the second part begins with a capital letter.
  • Quote within a quote: single quotation marks inside the double ones.
  • Capital letters: the first word of every sentence, “I”, and names of people, countries and days.
  • Exclamation marks: only for strong feeling such as surprise or excitement.
Worked example
that is true maya said but we must be careful
→ “That is true,” Maya said, “but we must be careful.”
